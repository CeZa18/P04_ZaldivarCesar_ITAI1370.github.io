ITAI-1370 Artificial Intelligence History

Summary page for the Introduction to Artificial Intelligence History class at Houston Community College.

Course Overview

This summary page collects the main themes and lessons from the ITAI-1370 course. The projects in this course examine the history of artificial intelligence, important milestones, and the impact of AI on society.

Project Themes

The project pages included in this site explore topics such as:

  • The origins of artificial intelligence and early pioneers
  • Key milestones in symbolic AI, expert systems, and search techniques
  • Machine learning breakthroughs and neural network history
  • Ethical questions, social impact, and AI responsibility
  • Modern developments and historical perspective on AI progress

How to Use This Summary

Use this page as a starting point to review the overall structure of the ITAI-1370 project portfolio. The individual pages in the project folder contain detailed research, report content, and reflections for each assignment.

About the Portfolio

This portfolio was created by Cesar for ITAI-1370 at Houston Community College. It demonstrates an understanding of artificial intelligence history through a combination of written summaries, research, and project documentation.
